# L003: finite Bernoulli trials for multiplicities

Zero fitted parameters. This law was derived after the L002 open demonstration,
before any region 2 census or summary was inspected. It is a standard
Poisson-binomial construction, not a new probability distribution or theorem
about Mersenne factors. No parameters are chosen from observed multiplicities.

## Derivation

Let w(p,k) be the unchanged supplied L001 Bateman–Horn/Kummer heuristic weight.
For each allowed k, the event that q=2kp+1 is a prime divisor occurs at most
once. Model its indicator as an independent Bernoulli random variable with
probability w(p,k). These weights are strictly between zero and one in the
declared prediction region; an invalid weight is an error, never clipped.
For the number J_p of census factors, the probability generating function is

    G_p(z) = product_{1<=k<=K} (1 - w(p,k) + w(p,k)*z).

Thus P(J_p=0)=product(1-w), P(J_p=1)=P(J_p=0)*sum(w/(1-w)),
E[#p with >=1]=sum_p(1-P(J_p=0)), and
E[#p with >=2]=sum_p(1-P(J_p=0)-P(J_p=1)). Coefficient convolution
gives the entire per-exponent distribution without Poissonizing it.
The mean remains sum_k w(p,k), exactly L001's mean. The variance is
sum_k w(p,k)*(1-w(p,k)), smaller by sum_k w(p,k)^2 than the Poisson variance.
This finite-trial correction has no fitted dispersion parameter.

Assuming independence across exponents, for M=max_p J_p,
P(M<=j)=product_p sum_{t=0}^j [z^t]G_p(z) and
E[M]=sum_{j>=0}(1-P(M<=j)). The implementation uses survival sums rather than
subtracting nearly equal cumulative probabilities. No observed maximum enters
the prediction. The truncation error is bounded using
P(J_p>=r)<=mu_p^r/r! and the union bound over exponents.

For fair comparisons L001 and L002 retain their old per-exponent Poisson
multiplicity assumptions. L003's total, bucket and residue expected counts are
identical to L001; only multiplicity predictions differ. The full summary
contains all three requested multiplicity cells, including the expected maximum.

## What is and is not proved

The generating-function formulas follow exactly from the independent Bernoulli
model. They do not establish independence of actual Mersenne factor events,
independence across p, or the L001 means. Arithmetic correlations can defeat
this model. Rejection is evidence against these assumptions; the same region
must not be reused after revising L003.

References: the operator-supplied L001 formula (preserved in the v0.2 source
snapshot); Yili Hong, *On computing the distribution function for the Poisson
binomial distribution*, CSDA 59 (2013), 41–51,
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.csda.2012.10.006. The method implemented here is direct
coefficient recursion, independently checked by enumerating small Bernoulli
outcome spaces. Numerical interpolation is a computational device, not fitting.
