# Supplied v0.2 L001 compatibility adapter

The original source and derivation are preserved without edits under
`archive/supplied-source/0.2/laws/`. This scalar adapter reproduces its `weight`
formula with the standard library, without NumPy or interpolation:

`1[L1] * 2*C2 * product((r-1)/(r-2), odd primes r|k) / (k*ln(2kp))`.

C2=0.6601618158468696 is the supplied approximation to the twin-prime constant.
The local factors follow the two-linear-polynomial Bateman--Horn heuristic:
for odd r not dividing k there are two roots to exclude; when r divides k
there is only one. L1 enforces the quadratic-residue constraint. The remaining
1/k is a residue-uniformity hypothesis, not a theorem conditional on p prime.
The formula has prior overlap with Shanks--Kravitz. It is not new in this build.

No archived census values were imported, and no claim of a fresh sealed test is
made for this already supplied model. Its historical commitments and results
remain in the original v0.2 ZIP, outside this build's preregistration chain.
The archived floating-point constant interval is provenance. This build also
recomputes a directed-rounding enclosure in `laws/constants_verified.json`,
using `python -m mf.constants`: C2 rounds to 0.660162 and 2C2 to 1.320324.
Its tail bound uses the larger sum over all integers, so does not assume a
prime-density theorem. The coarse heuristic normalization Lambda=1 follows
algebraically from averaging the divisor correction and allowed k classes;
equidistribution and the joint prime-quotient hypothesis remain assumptions.
The next experimental law is L002.
